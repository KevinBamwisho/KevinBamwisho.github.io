AlgoBowl

Algorithms competition, three-person team · Colorado School of Mines · Spring 2026

A solver for Enclose Horse, an NP-hard grid puzzle, built with a three-person team for a 47-team competition. I hand-authored a CP-SAT encoding on top of OR-Tools, a max-flow feasibility oracle, and a compute allocator that spent limited overnight compute where marginal rank gain was highest. It matched the provably optimal score on all 18 instances that could be checked and ranked #1 on 38 of 47 inputs.
